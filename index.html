01 / FUNDAMENTALS

Understand the SOTIF mindset.

Safety is not only about components failing. Sometimes a system operates as designed, but its intended functionality is insufficient in a foreseeable situation.

THE CORE QUESTION

What if nothing is broken,
but the function still isn't safe enough?

ISO 21448:2022 addresses unreasonable risk arising from functional insufficiencies, including limitations in function specification and E/E system performance. It covers verification, validation and relevant operational considerations.

ISO official scope ↗
21448ISO / 2022

FOUR BUILDING BLOCKS

From insufficiency to risk reduction

01

Intended functionality

Define what the feature should do and the conditions under which it is intended to operate.

AEB detects an obstacle and initiates braking.
02

Functional insufficiency

Identify a limitation or insufficient specification even when no hardware or software fault occurs.

Camera classification fails on low-contrast objects.
03

Triggering condition

Identify a scenario condition that exposes the limitation and may produce hazardous behavior.

Dusk, wet road glare, partial occlusion.
04

Risk reduction & evidence

Specify improvements, test cases, operational constraints and evidence to support a safety argument.

Targeted scenario tests and independent review.

DISTINCTION

ISO 26262 vs. ISO 21448

ISO 26262

Functional safety of road-vehicle E/E systems in relation to malfunctions and faults.

ISO 21448 / SOTIF

Hazards from inadequacies of intended functionality or its implementation, without relying on a malfunction.

They address different, complementary sources of risk; a real project may require both.

CASE STUDY · AEB-PEDESTRIAN-001

When the camera sees too late

At dusk, a pedestrian emerges near a crossing. Low contrast and reflections may delay classification, leaving insufficient distance to brake.

ANALYSIS RECORD

From limitation to test evidence

EXAMPLE ONLY · NOT VALIDATED
01 / FUNCTIONAutonomous Emergency Braking

Detect and respond to objects ahead to avoid or mitigate a collision within the system's specified operating domain.

02 / INSUFFICIENCYDelayed target recognition

The hypothetical perception pipeline has reduced contrast sensitivity and cannot confidently classify the pedestrian in time.

03 / TRIGGERDusk + glare + partial occlusion

Context may increase recognition uncertainty. No hardware or software malfunction is assumed.

04 / POTENTIAL HAZARDLate or absent braking

The vehicle may not slow sufficiently before reaching the pedestrian, potentially causing an impact.

05 / POSSIBLE MITIGATIONSImprove observability and response

Explore sensing/fusion changes, lower-confidence handling, an operating-domain restriction, and suitable alerting; verify effects.

06 / EVIDENCE PLANScenario-based validation

Define dusk lighting, pedestrian visibility and crossing variants; record acceptance criteria and test outcomes.
